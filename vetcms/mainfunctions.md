# User-Friendly Overview of Main Functions

The **VetCMS** system is designed to make life easier for veterinary professionals and clinic staff. Below, we explain the main features of the system in simple terms, so you can quickly understand how they can help you in your daily work.

---

## 1. User Management
- **What It Does**: This feature allows you to manage user accounts, including their roles, permissions, and associated pets.
- **How It Helps**: You can view and update user details, as well as access a list of pets owned by each user. This ensures that all user-pet relationships are accurately maintained.
- **Example**: If a user named "John Doe" owns "Buddy the dog," you can view and manage both the user's details and Buddy's record from the user management section.

---

## 2. Animal Type and Breed Management
- **What It Does**: This feature allows you to manage the types and breeds of animals in your clinic's database.
- **How It Helps**: You can add, update, or remove animal types and breeds to ensure accurate records for every patient.
- **Example**: If a new breed of dog, like "Australian Cobberdog," becomes common in your clinic, you can easily add it to the system.


---

## 3 Patient Management
- **What It Does**: This feature allows you to manage all patient records, including their ownership, type, and breed details.
- **How It Helps**: Think of it as a central hub where you can access and update all information about your patients. Ownership details are also managed here, ensuring that each patient is linked to the correct owner.
- **Example**: If "Buddy the dog" is adopted by a new owner or needs a correction in owner details, you can update it here.

---


## 4. Treatment Management
- **What It Does**: This feature helps you record and manage treatments provided to patients, including medications, procedures, and follow-ups.
- **How It Helps**: It ensures that all treatments are documented and easily accessible for future reference.
- **Example**: If "Buddy the dog" receives a rabies vaccine, you can log the treatment details, including the date and any follow-up requirements.

# Best Practices

- Regularly review and update the list of animal types and breeds to ensure it reflects the needs of your clinic.
- Verify patient ownership details during every visit to maintain accurate records.
- Document all treatments immediately after they are provided to ensure no details are missed.
- Periodically review user accounts to ensure roles and permissions are up-to-date.

---

# Troubleshooting Tips

- **Issue**: Unable to find a specific breed.
  - **Solution**: Check if the breed exists in the system. If not, add it to the database.
- **Issue**: Incorrect ownership details.
  - **Solution**: Update the patient's owner information in the "Ownership" section.
- **Issue**: Missing treatment records.
  - **Solution**: Ensure all treatments are logged immediately after they are provided.
- **Issue**: User cannot access their pets.
  - **Solution**: Verify the user's account and ensure their pets are correctly linked in the "Users" section.
